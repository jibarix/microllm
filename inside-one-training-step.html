Follow the number

A quick arithmetic refresher

1. A vector is a list of numbers

[2, 3]

In this model, every token is represented by a list of four numbers. The individual numbers do not have simple meanings like “noun” or “cat.”

2. Dot product: multiply, then add

[2, 3] · [4, 5]
= (2 × 4) + (3 × 5)
= 8 + 15 = 23

3. Matrix multiplication: row meets column

Take a row from the first grid and a column from the second. Their dot product becomes one output cell.

[2 3] × [4 1] = [23 8]
[5 2]

First cell: 2×4 + 3×5 = 23
Second cell: 2×1 + 3×2 = 8

The shared inner dimension must match: (3 × 4) multiplied by (4 × 4) produces (3 × 4).

4. Softmax: turn scores into shares

Exponentiate each score, then divide by their sum. Every result is positive and the results add up to one.

Scores [0, 0] → exp scores [1, 1]
Probabilities [1/2, 1/2] = [50%, 50%]

5. A gradient is a local sensitivity

If loss has gradient +0.3 with respect to a weight, increasing that weight by 0.001 would raise loss by approximately 0.0003, holding other weights fixed.

New weight = old weight − rate × gradient
0.20 − 0.10 × 0.30 = 0.17

A negative gradient makes the update increase the weight. The approximation is local; a very large step can make training worse.
